# 📊 Unlocking Business Insights: A Data Analysis of Munjal Electronics

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing the business operations of **Munjal Electronics**, a mobile retail store located in Abohar, Punjab. The objective is to identify inefficiencies in inventory management and provide data-driven recommendations to improve profitability and operational efficiency.

The analysis is based on real-world business data, covering sales, inventory, and payment behavior over a 6-month period (Nov 2024 – Apr 2025). :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify **inventory mismanagement issues**
- Analyze **brand-wise and price-wise sales performance**
- Understand **customer purchasing behavior**
- Evaluate **EMI vs Cash payment trends**
- Provide actionable insights for **better inventory planning**

---

## 🏪 About the Business
Munjal Electronics is a long-established offline mobile retail shop:
- 📍 Location: Abohar, Punjab
- 🗓️ Established: 1987
- 📱 Products: Smartphones, feature phones, accessories
- 💳 Services: EMI financing, SIM cards, recharges, resale devices :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

The business faces several operational challenges:

## 📈 Key Insights

### 📊 Sales Trends
- Sales peak observed in **January 2025**
- Revenue highest in **December 2024 (premium sales)** :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

### 💰 Price Segment Insights
- Budget (<₹10,000): High volume, low revenue
- Mid-Premium (₹30k–₹50k): High revenue driver
- Premium (>₹50k): Low volume, high profit

### 🏷️ Brand Performance
- **Samsung** → Highest revenue
- **Lava & Itel** → High volume (budget segment)
- **Oppo & Vivo** → Balanced performance :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

### 📦 Inventory Issues
- Overstocking across multiple brands
- Low stock turnover for some categories
- Capital tied in slow-moving products

### 💳 Payment Behavior
- Cash dominates overall sales
- EMI popular in mid-to-premium segments
- Bajaj Finserv is the leading EMI provider :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

### 📊 ABC Analysis
- A Category → ~68% revenue from few products
- C Category → ~79% products but low contribution

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Reduce stock of **low-performing (C category) products**
- Focus on **high-demand and high-margin segments**
- Improve **inventory forecasting**
- Strengthen **EMI partnerships**
- Promote **mid-premium products (₹30k–₹50k)**
